    Events and Personality¶

    Reactive Event List¶

    These are the event names you can put in the reactive field of a behavior JSON. When you do the matching action, a behavior for that event may cut into the current one (not every time; see How reactions play).

    The groups in the table are the same as the groups in the "Reactions" list in Control Deck › Stickman.

    Group Event name Trigger
    Working with files Worked You used a file or tool action that none of the rows below covers
    Working with files Created You created a new folder, a new text file, a new item or a shortcut
    Working with files Copied You copied, cut, pasted, moved or dragged and dropped
    Working with files Renamed You renamed something or ran a batch rename
    Working with files Deleted You deleted something
    Tidying and archiving Archived You compressed something
    Tidying and archiving Extracted You extracted or tested an archive
    Sharing and exporting Shared You did something to share, such as a share link, copying a Box path, or cloud or remote permissions
    Sharing and exporting Listed You exported or copied a list
    Looking around Looked You used tabs, preview, the view, the command palette, a context menu or quick jump, or switched panes
    Looking around Sorted You sorted
    Looking around ViewChanged You changed the view mode, icon size or column widths
    Looking around Inspected You looked inside something with Properties, hashes, compare, folder sync or Quick Look
    Looking around SearchStarted A search started
    Looking around SearchCompleted A search found results
    Looking around SearchEmpty A search found nothing
    Places and layout FolderNavigated You moved to another folder
    Places and layout TabSwitched You switched tabs
    Places and layout FavoriteAdded You added a favorite
    Places and layout NavJumped You switched the view in the nav pane
    Places and layout LayoutChanged You changed the nav pane layout, the window, or the toolbar state
    Places and layout WorkingSetSwitched You used a working set
    Tools AiUsed You used an AI feature (except talking to Stickman)
    Tools MacroRan You ran a macro
    Tools TerminalUsed You used the terminal (including opening a terminal tab and sending to Claude)
    Tools ThemeChanged You changed the theme
    Moments and milestones AppStartup The app started (once)
    Moments and milestones LevelUp You leveled up
    Moments and milestones FileOperationCompleted A file operation such as a copy, move or delete finished
    Moments and milestones Combo You repeated the same action (with gaps of 6 seconds or less: 8 times on Quiet, 5 on Normal, 3 on Lively)
    Moments and milestones LateNight You used the app between midnight and 5 a.m. (now and then, not every time)
    When things go wrong Oops You used undo or redo
    When things go wrong ErrorOccurred You cancelled an operation partway through

    Combo and LateNight are special reactions that play instead of the ordinary one. After one plays, there is a longer wait before the next special reaction (5 minutes on Quiet, 2 on Normal, 1 on Lively).

    How reactions play¶

    • Stickman does not react to every action. "Reaction frequency" in Control Deck › Stickman sets the chance that a reaction plays and the pause that follows it
    Reaction frequency Chance Pause after a reaction
    Quiet 15% 25 seconds
    Normal (the default) 35% 10 seconds
    Lively 80% 4 seconds

    Before the same reaction plays again, the pause is doubled. The higher the personality's reaction sensitivity, the more often reactions play and the shorter the pauses - AppStartup and LevelUp always play, with no pause - You can give one event as many behaviors as you like; one of them is picked each time - Behaviors you switched off in the "Reactions" list, and Pro behaviors in the free version, never play. If an event has no behavior that can play, nothing happens - While you are talking to Stickman, it does not react

    Personality¶

    Choose the personality under "Personality" in Control Deck › Stickman.

    Personality Share of basic behaviors Reaction sensitivity Speed Character
    Calm (calm) 70% Low (×0.5) ×0.7 Mostly basic behaviors; moves slowly and walks further
    Playful (playful) 30% Normal ×1.3 Mostly special behaviors; quick, with shorter walks
    Whimsical (random, the default) 50% Normal ×1.0 Balanced, standard speed
    Diligent (reactive) 60% High (×1.5) ×1.1 Reacts readily to what you do

    How behaviors are chosen¶

    • Between behaviors, Stickman walks a distance that suits its personality
    • For the next behavior, the personality's share of basic behaviors first decides between basic behaviors ("category": "basic") and special ones (everything else); one is then picked by weight within that group
    • Higher-tier behaviors are more likely to be picked (Tiers 1–3 ×1, 4–6 ×1.5, 7–9 ×2, 10 ×3)
    • Recently played behaviors are less likely (the last one ×0.1, the one before ×0.3, the one before that ×0.6)
    • Each time a behavior is picked, there is a 1% chance that a rare behavior (the bear sighting) plays instead (Reward Behaviors)
    • Reward behaviors you have not unlocked, behaviors you switched off, and Pro behaviors in the free version are never picked
