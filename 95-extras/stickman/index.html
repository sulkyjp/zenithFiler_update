    Mascot (Stickman)¶

    A stick figure (Stickman) and its companion wander along the top edge of the status bar. They walk, run, sleep, look around and so on as they please, and sometimes react to what you do, such as file operations or searches.

    Showing Stickman¶

    Stickman is hidden at first. Turn on "Show Stickman on status bar" in Control Deck › Stickman to bring it out.

    • Even when it is on, Stickman does not walk while "Turn off all effects (instant display)" is on in Control Deck › Effects, while automatic lightweight mode is active, or while Windows' "Show animations" is off (View Options)
    • While Control Deck is open, Stickman goes into its house to rest and comes back out when you close it

    Talk to Stickman¶

    Hover over Stickman and it stops where it is and says something. Click it while it is talking and an input box opens in the speech bubble so you can talk to it.

    • What it says first: a fixed line chosen for the time of day (morning, daytime, evening, night, late night) and what you just did (a run of file operations, something that went wrong, tidying, sharing, searching and so on). Hovering alone never calls the generative AI.
    • Talking: type in the box and press Enter. The generative AI writes a short reply, and Stickman acts it out (claps, jumps, walks, yawns and so on). You can also ask for one, such as "jump". The gesture is chosen from every move Stickman has, including moves added from the store and unlocked moves (but not moves you switched off in the list, Pro moves in the free version, or rare moves such as the bear sighting). The Enter that confirms Japanese input-method conversion does not send.
    • Only Stickman moves: talking to it never operates the app. It does not open files or change settings.
    • It remembers only the last three exchanges, and forgets them when the bubble closes.
    • Closing: Esc, the × button on the bubble, or a click outside the bubble. When it has only said its first line, it closes shortly after the mouse moves away. Stickman then walks on.
    • Limits: five times a day in the free version, unlimited with Pro. If generative AI is not set up yet, the bubble shows a link to the AI settings.
    • Turning it off: switch off "Talk to Stickman" in Control Deck › Stickman, and Stickman will no longer stop when you hover over it (on by default).
    • What is sent to the generative AI is described in the privacy policy.
    • Over a pane that is showing a terminal, the speech bubble can look cut off.

    The Stickman page¶

    Control Deck › Stickman contains the following:

    • Show Stickman on status bar: Shows or hides Stickman (off at first)
    • Show dog companion: Shows or hides the companion. Its form depends on the theme (Reward Behaviors)
    • Dumbo octopus in terminal: Swaps the companion for a dumbo octopus while a terminal is on screen
    • Talk to Stickman: Turns the "Talk to Stickman" feature above on or off
    • Personality: Calm, Playful, Whimsical or Diligent. Changes what Stickman tends to do and how fast it moves (Events and Personality)
    • Reaction frequency: Quiet, Normal or Lively. Sets how often Stickman reacts to what you do
    • Generate behavior with AI: Has the AI create a new behavior (GIF Export and AI Generation)
    • Behavior Patterns: The everyday behaviors. Each can be turned on or off, and hovering over a card previews its motion. The right-click menu offers "Edit" and "Upload to store" for behaviors made with AI, "Save as GIF" for any behavior that is not locked, and "Delete" for behaviors from the store or made with AI
    • Reactions: The behaviors that react to what you do, grouped by the kind of trigger. Each group can also be turned on or off as a whole (Events and Personality)
    • Open stickman folder: Opens the folder holding the behavior files. Add JSON files there to add behaviors (Writing Behavior JSON)
    • Achievement Rewards: The behaviors unlocked by completing challenges (Reward Behaviors)

    Stickman Store¶

    Control Deck › Stickman Store lets you find and add behaviors other people have made.

    • Search by name, author or description, filter by category (Basic (everyday), Reactive (event-triggered), Standard (mid-tier), Showcase (dramatic moves), Custom (user-submitted)), and sort by newest, rating, category or name
    • "Preview" makes the Stickman on your status bar perform it on the spot
    • "Get" adds it to Stickman's behaviors right away, no restart needed. Behaviors you already have are marked "Installed". You can switch them on or off, or delete them, under "Behavior Patterns" on the Stickman page
    • Rate behaviors from ★1 to ★5
    • "Upload" posts a behavior JSON of your own. Uploading unlocks when your challenge level reaches 50. Rating and uploading need the user ID you registered on the Theme Store page
    • "My Uploads" lists what you have posted; right-click and choose "Delete" to take one down

    How behaviors work¶

    • Behaviors are defined by JSON files in the data\stickman folder. They are all loaded at startup; a broken file is skipped on its own and the app starts normally
    • Stickman picks its next behavior from its personality, how likely each behavior is to be chosen (its weight), and what it did recently. The behavior it just did is less likely to come up again, so the same one does not keep repeating
    • A behavior is made of steps (phases), and each step sets the pose, speed, jumps and so on. The companion follows each step's instructions or simply trails Stickman
    • When you do something, a reacting behavior may cut into the current one (Events and Personality)
