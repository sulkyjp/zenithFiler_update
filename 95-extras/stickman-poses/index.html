    Poses, Dog Commands and Props¶

    Pose List¶

    These are the poses you can use in a phase's pose. All of them are built into the app and any behavior can use them. A name that is not in the list is drawn standing (stand).

    Basic

    Pose Description
    stand Standing upright (default)
    walk Walking
    run Running
    sleep Sleeping (the dog sleeps too)
    crouch Crouching low
    sit Sitting
    kneel Kneeling on one knee

    Dog Commands (dogBehavior)¶

    Setting dogBehavior on a phase gives the companion (dog, cat, robot and so on; its form depends on the theme) a special instruction. Without one, the companion follows Stickman and joins in on some of its poses by itself.

    Command Description
    null (omit) Normal follow. The companion follows behind the stick figure
    run_ahead Runs ahead in the direction of travel, then waits wagging
    come_pet Trots to the stick figure's side and sits, tail wagging (pair with the pet pose)
    circle_round Runs circles around the stick figure
    zoomies Dashes back and forth at top speed
    lead_the_way Trots ahead, looks back, then trots on (leading the way)
    heel Walks calmly right at the stick figure's side
    bark Hops in place excitedly, as if barking
    spin Spins rapidly in place (happy)
    play_bow Front low, rear up: an invitation to play
    chase_tail Spins chasing its own tail
    pounce Crouches, then pounces forward
    wiggle Wiggles its whole body with joy
    roll_over Rolls onto its back
    jump_from_head Runs over and leaps in a big arc, as if jumping off the stick figure's head
    somersault Imitates a somersault
    high_jump Springs high in place
    backflip Backflip
    leap A long bounding leap forward
    hop Keeps hopping in place
    dance Sways side to side, dancing
    beg Sits up with front paws raised, begging
    watch Sits and watches the stick figure
    lie_down Lies down at the stick figure's side (relaxed, submissive)
    sit_stay Sits still facing forward (stay)
    stretch A long stretch
    sniff_ground Wanders slowly, sniffing the ground
    dig Digs with its front paws
    scratch Sits and scratches itself
    alert Freezes with its head high, staring ahead
    shake_off Shakes its whole body

    There are also commands used only by the rare "Bear Sighting" event (transform_bear, roar_bear, chase_stickman, revert_to_dog). AI behavior generation never uses them (Reward Behaviors).

    Available Props (prop keys)¶

    Set "prop": "<key>" on a behavior phase to make a small prop fade in 40 px ahead of the Stickman (it stays where it appeared, and disappears when a later phase names a different prop or none). The same keys are usable from AI behavior generation. Prop text is always shown in Japanese, as in the table, whatever the display language.

    Key Display Typical scene
    coffee_cup ☕ いっぷく Break / idle
    umbrella ☂ Sad / rainy
    white_flag 🏳 降参 Surrender / overwhelmed
    boombox 🎵♪ Music vibe
    popcorn 🍿 Watching long process
    gold_medal 🥇 Achievement / completion
    broom 🧹 Cleanup / delete
    gift_box 🎁 Reward unlocked
    paper_airplane ✈ Sending file
    magic_wand ✨ 魔法 AI generation success
    hard_hat ⛑ 工事中 Indexing / under construction
    magnifier 🔍 Searching
    sleep_z 💤 zzz Long idle
    light_bulb 💡 ひらめき Tip / insight
    clipboard_check 📋 完了 Operation complete
    key_gold 🔑 解放 Level unlocked
    dumbbell 🏋 重い… Heavy operation
    boba_tea 🧋 Stylish break
    birthday_cake 🎂 おめでとう Anniversary
    caution_cone 🚧 作業中 Warning / editing
    bear_warning_sign ⚠ クマ出没注意 Rare event (bear chase)

    hard_hat, caution_cone and bear_warning_sign appear as yellow signs with a black border; the others appear on a white tag.
