    Theme List and Categories¶

    Categories¶

    The bundled themes are grouped into five categories. A category describes the theme's character, not how light or dark its background is. Standard and Lifestyle themes are all light, while Professional, Premium and Retro & Tech each include both light and dark themes.

    Category Character
    Standard Where Zenith Filer started. A basic set with the readability and versatility to fit any working environment
    Lifestyle Pastel and warm palettes that sit comfortably in everyday life, with café, floral and seasonal motifs
    Professional Palettes for focused, disciplined work. High contrast that suits long sessions of administration or coding
    Premium A luxurious set drawn from the beauty of gemstones and nature, lifting the tool a notch above the everyday
    Retro & Tech Hobbyist designs that recall vintage consoles and near-future cyberspace

    Themes you make yourself or generate with AI go under "My Themes", and themes you get from the Theme Store go under "Store". Writing a Category into a theme's JSON does not change this: unless the theme is one of the bundled ones, it lands in one of those two groups.

    Theme List¶

    The descriptions are the same ones shown on the theme cards in the app.

    Standard¶

    Theme Name Description
    standard Standard calm beige-toned theme for Zenith Filer
    Plain A plain, unadorned basic theme made only of white and light gray
    SolarizedLight A scientifically calculated color design for extended work sessions (high-visibility tuned edition)
    Nordic A crisp color scheme combining glacial cleanliness with serene silence
    PaperWhite A theme reproducing the contrast of paper and ink, specialized for organizing text information
    Daylight A neutral gray theme balancing the softness of natural light with everyday practicality
    CloudGray A clean, modern UI theme based on pure cool gray tones
    Snowfield A modern theme with vivid cobalt blue standing out against a snow-white background
    Limestone A warm stone-based theme reminiscent of limestone, accented with subdued amber
    SilverBirch A birch forest-inspired theme with light gray carrying a hint of green, accented by forest green
    Graphite A sketchbook-inspired theme with slightly dark warm gray and charcoal accents
    • The app starts with standard the first time you launch it.
    • Plain makes the app look close to Windows File Explorer. Beyond its colors, it draws no list grid lines, selection outlines, light lines or shadows, and the minimize, maximize and close buttons take the Windows shape.
